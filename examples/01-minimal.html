Riverside Bakery

A fictional website. Click the green button in the bottom right corner to open the chat.

Example 1: Minimal setup

One <script> tag is all you need. The widget adds a floating WhatsApp button. A click opens a chat window that looks and feels like WhatsApp. When the visitor presses “Send”, WhatsApp opens with the message already filled in.

<script src="https://wa-widget.doebeling.de/v2/whatsapp-widget.js"
        data-phone="+49 000 0000000"
        data-name="Riverside Bakery"
        data-status="Usually replies within an hour"
        data-welcome="Hi there! 👋 How can we help you?"
        defer></script>

Only data-phone is required. Use the international format with country code.

What happens with the data?

  • The widget does not send anything to a server, sets no cookies and loads no external files.
  • The message stays in the browser until the visitor presses “Send”.
  • “Send” opens https://wa.me/…?text=…. From this moment on, WhatsApp (Meta) processes the data.

The example uses the placeholder number +49 000 0000000. The small log in the bottom left corner shows the WhatsApp link instead of opening it.
