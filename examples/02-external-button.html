Example 2: Open the chat with your own buttons

Set data-launcher="false" to hide the floating button. Then add data-wa-open to any link or button on your page:

<!-- Works without JavaScript, too: the link goes straight to WhatsApp -->
<a href="https://wa.me/490000000000" data-wa-open>Ask us on WhatsApp</a>

<!-- The value is used as the prefilled message -->
<button data-wa-open="Hi, is the olive tree (1.5 m) in stock?">Ask about this product</button>

<script src="https://whatsapp-widget.doebeling.dev/whatsapp-widget.js"
        data-phone="+49 000 0000000"
        data-name="Green Thumb Garden Shop"
        data-welcome="Hello! Which plant are you interested in?"
        data-launcher="false"
        data-position="left"
        defer></script>
